# Focus Bear Company Reflection

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

I think Focus Bear was created to give people with ADHD or autism practical help with focus and routines. It can also help people who struggle with attention, whether or not they have a diagnosis. It gives people a way to build structure into their day so everyday tasks are easier to manage.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

Knowing what you need to do and actually starting it can be two different things. Getting started stands out to me because it’s easy to put off the first step or get sidetracked by another app. Guided routines make the next step easier to see, and distraction blocking makes it easier to stay with the task once you’ve started.

## Why do you think this mission is important?

These challenges can come up every day at work, at uni, or at home. Having to put extra effort into starting and remembering everyday tasks can be exhausting. I think it matters that Focus Bear helps people build routines they can keep up with without burning out.

## How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?

My background in psychology has helped me understand how attention difficulties can affect everyday life for people with ADHD or autism. I’m interested in how that understanding can inform the software we build. What I value about Focus Bear is that it turns that understanding into practical support people can use during their day.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

Yes. I procrastinate on tasks I know I need to do, and getting started is usually the hardest part for me. The AI feature on my Mac has helped me pause and ask whether what I’m opening is relevant to the task I’m trying to do. I also like that I can choose which apps to block. I’ve struggled to find a productivity app that syncs well between my Mac and iPhone, and Focus Bear seems to handle that well so far.
